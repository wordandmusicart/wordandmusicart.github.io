# Brief

## Purpose

Give visitors one clear, elegant destination while the full word&music website is being developed, and direct them to the nearest concert.

## Experience

- Refined editorial minimalism on white.
- The original black word&music logo appears first.
- “Наш найближчий концерт” uses the supplied Fixel Text typeface.
- The concert artwork is the sole call to action and opens Eventmate.
- Desktop uses the supplied 16:9 artwork; smartphone uses the supplied 4:5 artwork.
- Everything fits inside the visible viewport without page scrolling.
- Elements reveal softly in sequence, with a reduced-motion fallback.

## Success criteria

- Correct supplied assets and exact Eventmate URL.
- No crop or content substitution.
- Serve resolution steps for each poster according to rendered width and device pixel ratio, keeping the supplied full-resolution PNGs unchanged.
- No overflow at representative desktop and phone sizes.
- Keyboard-visible link focus and meaningful alternative text.
- Live through GitHub Pages at `https://wordandmusic.art/` (with `wordandmusicart.github.io` redirecting to it).

## Non-goals

- Navigation, ticket checkout, CMS, analytics, or the future full site.
