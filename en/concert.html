About

We invite you into the refined world of French music, where elegance of sound meets the power of the poetic word and an unmistakably enchanting atmosphere. Melodies in which time stands still, and feelings that stay forever.

The subtle French mélodies of Georges Bizet, Greek folk songs and the passionate Tzigane in Maurice Ravel’s artistic interpretation, and the ironic charm and tenderness of Francis Poulenc’s music.

The virtuoso energy of violin and piano intertwines with the voice and the spoken word. Music and poetry form a single story, experienced through each listener’s own perception of the world.

Stories of love and dreams, tenderness and passion, irony and sincerity — in feelings beyond the reach of time.

Three composers. Three artistic worlds. One musical journey beyond time.

An evening in which music touches the heart and every moment is filled with meaning.

Vivre, Aimer, rêver… Live. Love. Dream.

Programme
  1. Georges Bizet5 mélodies, op. 21:
    “April Song” — words by Louis Bouilhet; “The Calm Sea” — words by Alphonse de Lamartine; “Song of the Madman” and “The Arabian Woman’s Farewell” — words by Victor Hugo; “I Will Say Nothing of It” — words by Jules-Adenis de Colombeau.
  2. Maurice RavelRigaudon from the piano suite Le Tombeau de Couperin
    Solo piano.
  3. Maurice Ravel5 Greek Folk Songs
    Translation by M. Calvocoressi. “The Wedding Song”; “There, by the Church”; “What Gallant Can Compare with Me?”; “Song of the Mastic Gatherers”; “So Merry!”.
  4. Maurice RavelTzigane
    Concert rhapsody for violin and piano.
  5. Francis Poulenc
