About

Three words — three states of the soul. The French tenderness of Bizet and Ravel and the Spanish fire of de Falla, with poetry in between.

An evening of chamber music where romance, instrumental miniature and recitation form one story — about life, love and dreams.

In enchanted moments, when the flow of time comes to a halt and music carries us to where dreams are born. To a world where the poetic word intertwines with melody, folk inflections come alive in a new sound, and feelings unfold with exceptional emotional depth.

“Vivre, Aimer, Rêver...” is a concert programme about feelings and images that exist beyond time. About love and dreams, passion and tenderness, freedom and solitude, where true art is born.

In Georges Bizet’s music, French poetry takes on extraordinary emotional force. His chamber miniatures are subtle psychological sketches in which transparent lyricism gradually gives way to dramatic tension, and each poetic image reveals an entire world of human experience.

Maurice Ravel takes us into a realm of refined colours, rhythms and exotic imagery. In “Five Greek Folk Melodies”, the simplicity of folk song meets impeccable French refinement: the solemnity of a wedding ceremony, the quiet space beside an old church, playfulness and passion come alive in delicate musical miniatures.

In the rhapsody “Tzigane”, a free, spirited, dazzling world appears before us, where virtuosity becomes the language of passion, inner freedom and elemental energy.

In Manuel de Falla’s “Seven Spanish Folk Songs”, the music is filled with a particular sensuality: sunlight gives way to evening stillness, dance rhythms to the sustained lines of folk song, and ardour to restraint. The breath of Spain can be felt in every phrase and pause.

Three composers. Three distinct artistic worlds. One journey beyond time.

On this musical journey, French lyricism meets Greek folklore, and Spanish temperament meets French refinement.

And perhaps, for a brief moment, time will truly cease to matter.

Vivre, Aimer, Rêver...

Moments beyond time.
