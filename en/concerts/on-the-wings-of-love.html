About the concert

The “On the Wings of Love” concert, part of the international word&music project, is an unforgettable encounter with vocal masterpieces from Ukraine and around the world, performed by Ukrainian opera stars — People’s Artists of Ukraine Anzhelina Shvachka and Liliia Hrevtsova — together with their students — a new generation of Ukrainian opera singers.

International and Ukrainian competition laureates also take part in the concert.

Art unites, art heals — don’t wait! Bright impressions and a wonderful mood await you!

Programme

Works by Wolfgang Amadeus Mozart, Robert Schumann, Gaetano Donizetti, Georges Bizet, Jacques Offenbach, Henri Duparc, Vincenzo Di Chiara, Claude Debussy, Francesco Cilea, George Gershwin, alongside a kaleidoscope of Ukrainian classical art songs and folk songs arranged by outstanding Ukrainian composers.
